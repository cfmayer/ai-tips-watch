Automating anything: start with the goal, not the process

Brain-dump the process, then make the bot restate it before acting

From the same article: voice-dictate everything about how you do the job: the usual steps, what you watch out for, the apps and tools used, how you check the work, where the inputs come from, and where feedback comes from.
