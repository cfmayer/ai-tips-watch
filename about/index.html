About

AI Tips Watch collects practical tips about Grok Bot and other AI tools (coding agents, Cursor, Claude Code, Codex, agent skills, prompting and workflows) from a fixed list of builders on X: @poteto, @mattpocockuk, @owickstrom, @EnoReyes, @dexhorthy, @cursor_ai, @ccheever, @ericzakariasson, @Baconbrix, @swyx, @jkup, @ajambrosino, @GeoffreyHuntley, @zachlloydtweets, @aninibread, @SIGKITTEN.

How it works

  • A daily run reads these accounts' recent public posts (plus threads, quoted posts and linked videos), keeps only practical AI-tooling content, and merges new tips into data/tips.json, deduplicated by source.
  • Tips are paraphrased, not quoted verbatim, and every one links to its exact source post, article or video (with a timestamp when it comes from a video). When an account shares someone else's post, the original author is credited.
  • Nothing is invented: no numbers or claims appear that aren't in the source. Video summaries are added only after a video has been watched; summaries marked "from transcript" were written from auto-captions only.
  • Off-topic posts are skipped.
